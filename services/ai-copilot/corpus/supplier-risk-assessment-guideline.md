# Supplier Risk Assessment Guideline

Defines how ProcureIQ assigns and reviews the `riskRating` field on a
Supplier record (LOW / MEDIUM / HIGH) and what each tier means operationally.

## Risk tiers

**LOW** — Supplier has a multi-year track record with ProcureIQ, no open
Code of Conduct issues, financially stable (per the most recent credit check),
and operates in a low-geopolitical-risk jurisdiction. Reviewed annually.

**MEDIUM** — Default rating for a newly onboarded supplier, or a supplier
with a minor unresolved issue (a single late delivery, a pending
documentation request) that doesn't rise to a compliance violation.
Reviewed every 6 months.

**HIGH** — Supplier has an open Code of Conduct concern, operates in a
jurisdiction with elevated export-control or sanctions complexity, has failed
a financial stability check, or has had two or more late/incomplete
deliveries in the trailing 12 months. Reviewed quarterly. HIGH-rated
suppliers are automatically moved to prepayment or Net 15 payment terms per
the Payment Terms Policy, and any Purchase Requisition naming a HIGH-risk
supplier should route through DIRECTOR-level approval regardless of the
requisition's dollar amount.

## What changes a rating

A rating change requires one of: a completed compliance review, a financial
stability re-check, a documented delivery-performance pattern (not a single
incident), or a geopolitical/regulatory change affecting the supplier's
operating jurisdiction. A rating should never be changed solely because a
requisitioner wants to avoid the HIGH-risk approval routing — that is itself
treated as a Code of Conduct-adjacent concern if it happens.

## Escalation

Any HIGH-risk supplier involved in a Purchase Order above $50,000 should be
flagged to the CFO's office regardless of the standard MANAGER/DIRECTOR/CFO
approval-amount routing, as an additional notification, not a replacement for
that routing.
