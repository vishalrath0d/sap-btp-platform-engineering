# Supply Agreement Summary — Global Circuit Supply (Supplier S3)

Reference contract for Global Circuit Supply, a MEDIUM-risk India-based
supplier of packaging and general procurement materials.

## Term and scope

Covers packaging materials and general indirect-procurement goods, ordered
via individual Purchase Orders referencing agreement number GCS-2025-0088.
Initial term is 12 months from signature, renewable by mutual written
agreement — no automatic renewal clause, unlike the Acme agreement.

## Payment terms

**Net 30**, paid in USD. No early payment discount is offered under this
agreement — any early-payment request must be negotiated per purchase order,
it is not a standing contract term.

## Delivery SLA

Global Circuit Supply commits to a 10 business day lead time for standard
packaging materials from Purchase Order acknowledgement. There is no
committed SLA for non-standard/custom packaging — those are quoted per order.

## Liability cap

Total liability is capped at 100% of the value of the specific Purchase Order
giving rise to the claim. There is no separate higher cap for gross negligence
in this agreement — unlike Acme's agreement, this is a flat cap regardless of
cause, which Procurement should account for when sizing orders against this
supplier for higher-risk materials.

## IP ownership

Any packaging design work commissioned from Global Circuit Supply as part of
a Purchase Order is work-for-hire: ProcureIQ owns the resulting designs
outright upon payment.

## Termination

Either party may terminate for convenience with 30 days' written notice —
shorter than the Acme agreement's 90-day notice period. ProcureIQ may
terminate immediately for a confirmed Code of Conduct violation.

## Quality standard

Materials must meet the specification stated on the Purchase Order. Nonconforming
shipments may be rejected within 15 days of receipt for a full replacement or
refund at Global Circuit Supply's cost.
