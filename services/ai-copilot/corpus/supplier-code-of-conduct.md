# ProcureIQ Supplier Code of Conduct

All suppliers onboarded through ProcureIQ must accept and comply with this Code
of Conduct as a condition of doing business. This document is referenced in
every Purchase Order's standard terms.

## Labor standards

Suppliers must not use forced, bonded, or child labor at any tier of their own
supply chain. Working hours must comply with local law and must not exceed 60
hours per week except in documented, exceptional circumstances. Suppliers must
provide a safe working environment and carry appropriate workers' compensation
or equivalent coverage for all employees involved in fulfilling ProcureIQ
purchase orders.

## Environmental compliance

Suppliers manufacturing physical goods must hold valid environmental permits
for their operating jurisdiction and must not knowingly violate local
emissions, waste-disposal, or hazardous-materials regulations. Suppliers
shipping internationally must comply with all applicable export-control and
customs regulations.

## Anti-bribery and conflicts of interest

Suppliers must not offer gifts, kickbacks, or any other form of improper
inducement to ProcureIQ employees involved in the requisition, approval, or
purchase order process. Any supplier employee with a personal or familial
relationship to a ProcureIQ approver must disclose that relationship before
a Purchase Order is issued. Violation of this section is grounds for
immediate supplier status change to BLOCKED and termination of open Purchase
Orders.

## Data protection

Suppliers handling ProcureIQ or ProcureIQ-customer data as part of fulfilling
an order must implement reasonable technical and organizational security
measures and must notify ProcureIQ Procurement Operations within 72 hours of
discovering any data breach affecting that data.

## Consequences of non-compliance

A confirmed Code of Conduct violation results in one of the following, at
Procurement's discretion based on severity:
- Supplier risk rating raised to HIGH and a mandatory quarterly review cycle
- Supplier status changed to INACTIVE (no new Purchase Orders until remediated)
- Supplier status changed to BLOCKED (existing open Purchase Orders reviewed
  for cancellation) for severe or repeated violations
