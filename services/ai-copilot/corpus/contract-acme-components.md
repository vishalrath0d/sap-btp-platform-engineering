# Supply Agreement Summary — Acme Components Ltd (Supplier S1)

Reference contract for Acme Components Ltd, a LOW-risk US-based supplier of
industrial automation components (PLC modules and related hardware).

## Term and scope

This agreement covers the supply of industrial control-system components as
ordered via individual Purchase Orders referencing this agreement number
(ACME-2025-0142). The agreement runs for 24 months from signature with an
automatic 12-month renewal unless either party gives 90 days' written notice.

## Payment terms

**Net 30**, with a 2/10 early payment discount option (2% off if paid within
10 days). Payments are made in USD.

## Delivery SLA

Acme commits to shipping in-stock items within 3 business days of Purchase
Order acknowledgement, and custom/non-stock items within 15 business days.
A delivery more than 5 business days late against the committed ship date
counts as a late-delivery event for risk-rating purposes.

## Liability cap

Acme's total liability under this agreement, for any single incident, is
capped at the greater of $50,000 or 100% of the value of the Purchase Order
giving rise to the claim — except for claims arising from Acme's gross
negligence or willful misconduct, which are uncapped.

## Termination

Either party may terminate for convenience with 90 days' written notice.
ProcureIQ may terminate immediately for a confirmed Code of Conduct violation
or for Acme's insolvency.

## Quality warranty

All components are warranted against manufacturing defects for 12 months from
delivery. Defective units are replaced at Acme's cost, including shipping.
